---

## 🎯 Golden Rules for the Presenters (قواعد ذهبية للمُلقين)
1. **Keep it Simple & Short:** Don't speak fast or use complicated words. The simpler the English, the better the judges understand!
2. **One sentence per slide is enough:** Say the key idea with confidence and a smile.
3. **Point to the Screen:** When demonstrating roads, cars, or code, point to the screen so judges see it live.
