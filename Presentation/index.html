Why We Made Turbo

A friend had an accident because of a road sign. We wanted to help!

📍 Stadium Street, Kafr El Sheikh, Egypt • شارع الاستاد، كفر الشيخ

A friend got hurt in a car crash because he didn't know what the road sign meant. We decided to build a fun car game so kids learn road signs before they get hurt!
